Explore the city

The city that never sleeps

From bright Broadway lights to peaceful park paths, New York City brings together iconic sights, culture, food, and stories from around the world.

Start your visit in Manhattan, where world-famous landmarks sit alongside neighborhood cafés, museums, and lively streets. Take in harbor views, wander through Central Park, and discover something new in every borough.

A few must-see attractions

  1. Statue of Liberty — a welcoming symbol of freedom in New York Harbor.
  2. Times Square — bright signs, theaters, and the energy of Midtown.
  3. Central Park — a green escape in the heart of Manhattan.
  4. Empire State Building — an Art Deco icon with sweeping city views.
  5. Brooklyn Bridge — a memorable walk above the East River.
